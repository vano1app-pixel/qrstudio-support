QR Studio — Privacy

Data collected: none.

QR Studio works entirely on your iPhone. It does not have accounts, analytics, tracking, advertising or a server. Files and recordings never leave the device unless you share them yourself with the share sheet.

Purchases

Paid features are sold by Apple as an in-app purchase or subscription. Apple handles the payment and the receipt — QR Studio only reads whether your purchase is valid. You can restore purchases in the app and manage or cancel subscriptions in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Permissions

QR Studio asks for the camera only when you open the scanner. Making codes needs no permission.
